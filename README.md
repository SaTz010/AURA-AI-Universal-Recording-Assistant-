# AURA — Context-Aware Audio Intelligence System

**AURA (Audio Understanding & Recording Assistant)** is a context-aware audio intelligence system designed to transform unstructured spoken audio into structured, meaningful information. The system aims to bridge the gap between raw audio recordings and actionable insights by applying speech recognition and natural language processing techniques.

## Project Overview

AURA enables users to capture or upload voice-based audio and automatically convert it into organized textual outputs. By leveraging speech-to-text processing and context-aware language analysis, the system extracts essential information and presents it in a structured format to improve accessibility, comprehension, and usability of audio content.

The project focuses on real-world audio scenarios such as meetings, lectures, and personal voice notes, where large amounts of spoken information must be processed efficiently.

## Core Objectives

- Enable recording or uploading of voice-based audio from mobile devices  
- Apply speech recognition techniques to accurately transcribe audio content  
- Utilize context-aware natural language processing tailored to user-specified audio contexts  
- Extract meaningful insights from transcribed audio

## System Outputs

The system processes audio recordings and generates:

- **Structured summaries** of the recorded content  
- **Key takeaways** highlighting the most important information  
- **Topic-wise segmentation** to organize discussions into logical sections  
- **Formatted PDF reports** for improved readability and documentation  

## Application Contexts

AURA is designed to support multiple real-world use cases, including:

- Academic lectures and study sessions  
- Business meetings and discussions  
- Personal voice notes and idea capture  

By transforming spoken information into structured knowledge, AURA aims to enhance productivity, knowledge management, and information accessibility.
